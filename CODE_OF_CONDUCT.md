# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards for Technical Rigor

This project is a scientific and technical decompilation effort:
- Verify before asserting: All reverse engineered offsets, functions, and data structures must be grounded in actual ROM assembly or disassembler output.
- No destructive or undocumented patches: Modifications to tools or reconstructed source must be documented and reproducible.
- Clean commit hygiene: Follow conventional commit guidelines (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `refactor:`, `chore:`). Never include auto-generated attribution lines or noise.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
